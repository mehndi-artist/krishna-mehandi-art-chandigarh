Booking & Application Guidelines

Please read these simple guidelines to ensure a smooth experience and the best possible stain.

  • 1
    Advance Booking: A token advance is required to confirm your date and time slot. This secures your booking and allows us to prepare fresh organic henna.
  • 2
    Pre-Application Prep: Please ensure your hands and feet are washed cleanly with soap and are completely dry. Do not apply any lotions, creams, or oils before the mehndi application.
  • 3
    Comfortable Seating: Bridal mehndi takes several hours. Please arrange a comfortable seating area with good lighting and normal room temperature.
  • 4
    Aftercare is Crucial: Leave the paste on for at least 8-10 hours. Avoid washing with water for the first 24 hours. Apply mustard oil or balm after scraping off the dried paste for the darkest stain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. If you need more details, feel free to contact us directly.

1. How far in advance should I book my bridal mehndi in Chandigarh?
We strongly recommend booking your bridal mehndi at least 2 to 3 months in advance. During the peak wedding seasons (October to March), dates fill up quickly, so early booking secures your preferred time.
2. Do you travel to the venue for mehndi services in Chandigarh and Tricity?
Yes, we provide premium home and venue services. Our team travels across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, and Zirakpur for bridal bookings and group events.
3. Is your henna 100% natural and chemical-free?
Absolutely. Our henna paste is homemade using pure, premium organic Rajasthani henna leaves, eucalyptus oil, and lemon. We never use PPD, dyes, or harmful chemicals.
4. How can I ensure a deep and dark mehndi stain for my wedding?
Keep the organic paste on for 8 to 12 hours. Once dry, dab a lemon-sugar mixture on it. After scraping it off naturally, apply mustard oil or a soothing balm and avoid contact with water for at least 24 hours.
5. How much time does a full bridal mehndi application take?
Depending on the detail and length (usually up to the elbows and mid-calves), bridal mehndi application takes anywhere from 4 to 6 hours.
6. What is the starting price for bridal mehndi packages at Krishna Mehandi Art?
Our elegant, minimal bridal packages start at ₹3,100. For classic heavy bridal looks, the price starts at ₹5,100. We maintain complete transparency in our pricing.
7. Do you provide guest and party mehndi services for Sangeet ceremonies?
Yes, we have a dedicated and professional team of artists who can efficiently cater to large gatherings and apply beautiful designs for your guests at Sangeet or Mehendi functions.
8. Can I customize my bridal mehndi design with portraits or specific motifs?
Yes, customization is our specialty! From intricate bride and groom portraits to storytelling motifs like skylines or traditional Doli scenes, we design exactly what you envision.
9. Should I wash or moisturize my hands before the mehndi application?
Please wash your hands and feet thoroughly with soap and water to remove dirt. Do not apply any lotions, oils, or creams, as they create a barrier that prevents the henna from staining properly.
10. Is an advance booking amount required to secure my date?
Yes, a token advance deposit is required to lock in your date and time. This allows us to reject other inquiries for that slot and prepare fresh organic henna exclusively for your event.
