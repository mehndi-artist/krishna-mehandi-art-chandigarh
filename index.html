Booking & Application Guidelines

Please read these simple guidelines to ensure a smooth experience and the best possible stain.

  • 1
    Advance Booking: A token advance is required to confirm your date and time slot. This secures your booking and allows us to prepare fresh organic henna.
  • 2
    Pre-Application Prep: Please ensure your hands and feet are washed cleanly with soap and are completely dry. Do not apply any lotions, creams, or oils before the mehndi application.
  • 3
    Comfortable Seating: Bridal mehndi takes several hours. Please arrange a comfortable seating area with good lighting and normal room temperature.
  • 4
    Aftercare is Crucial: Leave the paste on for at least 8-10 hours. Avoid washing with water for the first 24 hours. Apply mustard oil or balm after scraping off the dried paste for the darkest stain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. If you need more details, feel free to contact us directly.

1. How far in advance should I book my bridal mehndi?
We recommend booking your bridal mehndi at least 2 to 3 months in advance, especially during the peak wedding season (October to March), to ensure your preferred dates and timings are secured.
2. Do you travel to the venue in Chandigarh?
Yes, we provide professional home and venue services across Chandigarh, Mohali, Panchkula, and Zirakpur for bridal bookings and group events. Travel charges may apply depending on the exact location.
3. Is your mehndi 100% natural and organic?
Absolutely! We strictly use 100% natural and organic henna. We mix premium Rajasthani henna powder with essential oils (like eucalyptus) and lemon juice. We never use chemical dyes or "fast-color" cones.
4. How can I get a darker mehndi stain?
To achieve a deep stain, leave the paste on for 8 to 12 hours. Dab a mixture of lemon juice and sugar once it dries to keep it stuck to the skin. After scraping it off, apply mustard oil or Vicks Vaporub and avoid soap/water for at least 24 hours.
5. How much time does bridal mehndi application take?
A full bridal mehndi (hands up to elbows and feet up to calves) generally takes about 4 to 6 hours depending on the intricacy of the design. We advise scheduling it on a day when you can relax completely.
6. Do you have a team for guest mehndi?
Yes, we have a skilled team of junior and senior artists who can handle guest mehndi efficiently for Sangeet, Mehendi ki Raat, and corporate events.
7. What is the starting price for bridal mehndi?
Our bridal packages start at ₹3,100 for elegant, minimal designs. Prices scale based on the length and detail of the design. Check our pricing section or contact us for a custom quote.
8. Can I customize my mehndi design?
Definitely! We encourage brides to share their references, Pinterest boards, or personal love stories. We love incorporating custom elements like skylines, pets, or specific motifs into the design.
9. Do you do portrait or figure mehndi?
Yes, we specialize in intricate portrait mehndi, including bride and groom faces, traditional Doli, Baraat scenes, and religious figures like Radha-Krishna.
10. How do I book an appointment?
You can easily book by filling out the form on this website, calling us directly, or sending a message on WhatsApp at +919530844861. We will discuss your requirements and share payment details for the advance deposit.
11. Should I wash my hands before the mehndi application?
Yes, please wash your hands and feet thoroughly with soap and water to remove any dirt or natural skin oils. Do not apply body lotion, moisturizers, or oils before we begin, as they create a barrier that prevents the henna from staining properly.
12. What happens if I have sensitive skin?
Since our henna is 100% organic and free of PPD and chemical dyes, it is generally very safe for sensitive skin. However, if you have allergies to specific essential oils (like eucalyptus), please inform us in advance so we can prepare a special paste for you.
13. Do you offer mehndi for festivals like Karwa Chauth and Diwali?
Yes, we offer specialized festival packages. Due to high demand during Karwa Chauth and Diwali, we operate on a pre-booking basis and also accept walk-ins at our Sector 15C studio based on availability.
14. Is an advance deposit required?
Yes, a non-refundable advance deposit of 20% to 30% is required to block your date and time. The remaining balance is to be paid on the day of the event after the application is complete.
15. What if I need to cancel or reschedule?
If you need to reschedule, please inform us at least 14 days in advance, and we will try to accommodate the new date based on availability. Cancellations will result in the forfeiture of the advance deposit.
