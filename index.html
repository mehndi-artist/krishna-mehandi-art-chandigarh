Mehndi Guidelines & Aftercare

Follow these expert tips to ensure you achieve the darkest, most beautiful stain for your special day.

What To Do

  • Book your appointment at least 2 months in advance.
  • Wash your hands and feet thoroughly with soap before application.
  • Ensure waxing and manicures/pedicures are completed a day prior.
  • Leave the mehndi paste on for a minimum of 8 to 12 hours.
  • Apply lemon-sugar mixture once the design is completely dry.
  • Apply mustard oil or Vicks vaporub after scraping off the crust.

What Not To Do

  • Do not apply moisturizers, lotions, or oils before application.
  • Do not wash off the mehndi paste with water; gently scrape it off.
  • Avoid contact with soapy water for at least 24 hours post-removal.
  • Avoid direct air conditioning blowing on wet henna.
  • Do not schedule a body spa or heavy workout right after application.
